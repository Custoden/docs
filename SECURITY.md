# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately. Do not open a public issue.

* **GitHub:** use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Custoden/docs/security/advisories/new) on any Custoden repository.
* **Email:** security@custoden.com

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. We acknowledge reports within 3 business days and keep you updated until the issue is resolved.

## Scope

* The Custoden API at `api.custoden.com`
* custoden.com and docs.custoden.com
* Public repositories in the [Custoden](https://github.com/Custoden) organization

Out of scope: denial of service, rate limit testing at volume, social engineering, and reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.

## Detection gaps

If the API misses a malicious package or workflow, or flags a legitimate one, that is not a vulnerability. Open a regular issue on [Custoden/docs](https://github.com/Custoden/docs/issues) with the package or workflow, and say whether it should be private.

## Safe harbor

We will not take legal action against research that follows this policy, avoids privacy violations and service disruption, and gives us reasonable time to fix an issue before disclosure.
